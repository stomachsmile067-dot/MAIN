## 🧩 4. Structural / Format Patterns

Each XO2 video follows the same skeleton:

1. **Cold open teaser (5–10 sec):** a single dramatic clip + a question.
2. **Sponsor / self-promo (5 sec):** plug email or sponsor like LagoFast.
3. **Story 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 (each 1.5–4 min):** Standalone mini-stories, each with a hook line, the clip, and Arabic voiceover explaining.
4. **Cliffhanger transitions** between stories ("بس اللي صار بعدها كان...").
5. **End screen (last 30 sec):** Outro + 2 video recommendations + subscribe CTA.

**Storytelling style:** He always adds context (background, what happened before, what happened after, the consequence). It's NOT just clips — it's **mini-documentaries** stitched together.

**Length sweet spot by content type:**
- Raid / hacker / near-death on stream: **8–9 min** (shorter punchier)
- Compilation lists (15 times, 10 moments): **15–17 min** (longer because more entries)
- Exploration / dangerous places: **11–14 min** (need scene-setting)
