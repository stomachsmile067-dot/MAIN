**Color:** Use 2-3 max
- ✅ Yellow text (high CTR)

- ✅ White/Black (clarity)
- ❌ Too many colors = mess

**Text:**
- ✅ 3-5 words MAX in Arabic
- ✅ Big, bold, with black outline
- ✅ Readable on mobile (50% of viewers)
- ❌ Don't repeat the title word-for-word

**Face:**
- ✅ Speed's face MUST be visible (faces = +30% CTR)

**Background:**
- ✅ High contrast (dark vs bright)
- ✅ Slight blur to focus on text/face
- ❌ Busy backgrounds
