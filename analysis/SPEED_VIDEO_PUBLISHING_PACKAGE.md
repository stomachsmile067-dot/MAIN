  - Best quality

### Step-by-Step (Canva):
```
1. Go to canva.com
2. Click "Create Design" → "YouTube Thumbnail"
3. Size: 1280x720 (auto)
4. Background: Solid color OR Speed image
5. Add text: "من مشرد لملك الإنترنت"
6. Font: Bold Arabic (Tajawal, Cairo, or similar)
7. Add Speed face (upload image)
8. Add elements:
   - Question marks
   - Trophy emoji 🏆
   - "?" marks
9. Adjust colors for high contrast
10. Download as PNG
```

### ⚠️ Thumbnail Rules:
- ✅ **High contrast** (yellow + black works best)
- ✅ **Big text** (readable on phone)
- ✅ **One clear subject** (Speed's face)
- ✅ **Not too much text** (5-7 words max)
- ❌ **Don't use clickbait** (YouTube might demonetize)
